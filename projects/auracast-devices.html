01Overview

Auracast is the broadcast feature of Bluetooth LE Audio. One transmitter streams audio to any number of nearby receivers, such as headphones, hearing aids or speakers, without pairing each of them.

At OpenEcore I built the devices behind it: the transmitter that sends the broadcast, the receiver that listens to it, and the assistant device that helps receivers find and join a stream.

The audio path inside these devices runs over I2S/TDM and the LC3 codec. The hardware is based on the STM32WBA55G and the NXP NXH3675, and I scaled the audio from mono to four channels.

02How it works

  • Audio sourceI2S / TDM · 4 channels
  • LC3 encoderCompresses the audio
  • TransmitterBLE Audio broadcast
  • ReceiversDecode and play out

  1. Audio comes in

    Audio enters the transmitter over I2S/TDM. TDM carries several channels on one data line, which is how the path grew from mono to four channels.

  2. LC3 encoding

    The LC3 codec compresses the audio for Bluetooth LE with low latency and low power.

  3. Broadcast

    The transmitter broadcasts the stream. No pairing is needed, so any number of receivers in range can listen.

  4. Find and join

    Receivers, with help from assistant devices, find the broadcast, sync to it, decode the audio and play it.
