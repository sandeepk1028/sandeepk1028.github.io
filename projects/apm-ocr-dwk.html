01Overview

At Factana Computing I worked on three commercial device lines: APM, DWK and OCR. These are products for customers, so hardware, firmware and validation all had to be dependable.

My work spanned hardware, firmware and validation: designing schematics and PCB layouts, keeping the bill of materials in order, and validating the finished devices.

To speed up testing, I built a custom RS232 test kit that shortened device connectivity validation.
