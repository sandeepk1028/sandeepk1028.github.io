01Overview

Driving after drinking, or while drowsy, puts the driver and everyone else at risk. This system watches for both while the vehicle is in use.

It monitors alcohol and the driver's alertness in real time, and takes automatic safety actions when the limits are crossed.

I developed it as an Embedded Engineer Intern at Pi Square Technologies.

02How it works

  • Alcohol sensorMeasures alcohol
  • Alertness sensorWatches for drowsiness
  • MicrocontrollerChecks the limits
  • Safety actionAlert and automatic response

  1. Sense

    Sensors measure alcohol and the driver's alertness continuously.

  2. Evaluate

    The microcontroller compares the readings with the safety limits.

  3. Act

    When a limit is crossed, the system takes an automatic safety action.
