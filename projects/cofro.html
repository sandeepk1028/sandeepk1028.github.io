01Overview

Picking coffee beans by hand is slow, repetitive work. COFRO moves that process from manual work to a semi-automated system.

It is a robotic coffee bean plucking machine that sees with a camera, recognises beans with an AI model, and uses embedded control to act on what it sees.

I developed its embedded hardware and firmware and integrated the AI camera models with the microcontrollers for real-time bean detection.

Manual → semi-autothe way coffee beans are plucked

02How it works

  • CameraCaptures the plant
  • AI modelDetects beans in real time
  • MicrocontrollerEmbedded control
  • Plucking mechanismPicks the beans

  1. See

    A camera captures the coffee plant in front of the machine.

  2. Detect

    An AI vision model finds the beans in the image in real time.

  3. Decide

    The microcontroller turns each detection into a control action.

  4. Pluck

    The mechanism plucks the beans, moving the work from manual to semi-automated.
