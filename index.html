About me

Sandeep Kumar K,
Embedded Engineer

I'm an Embedded Engineer with 2+ years of experience in firmware development, board bring-up, hardware-software integration, and system validation. Proficient in C, C++, Embedded C, and Python. I work across wireless audio, semi-automated robotics, and commercial embedded products.

My primary focus is Bluetooth audio, developing Auracast transmitters, receivers, and assistant devices using STM32WBA55G and NXH3675 chipsets, with I2S/TDM audio integration, LC3, A2DP, AAC codec streaming, and multi-channel audio configurations. I also contribute to SkyAmour, an Android application that extends Auracast capabilities to smartphones without native support.

My experience also includes semi-automated robotics projects such as COFRO and Smart Gardener, commercial device development (APM, DWK, and OCR), and a driver safety system for alcohol and drowsiness detection. I use oscilloscopes, logic analyzers, and JTAG/SWD debuggers for troubleshooting, firmware debugging, and hardware validation, with a focus on building reliable, practical embedded solutions.
