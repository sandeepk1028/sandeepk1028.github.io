About

I'm an embedded engineer with 2+ years of experience in firmware development, board bring-up and system validation. I work mostly in Embedded C and Python, close to the hardware.

My core area is wireless audio. I build Bluetooth Classic, BLE Audio and Auracast devices, and I've scaled I2S/TDM audio paths from mono to four channels. I also built SkyAmour, an Android app that brings Auracast to phones without native support.

Before audio, I worked on robotics and commercial devices. That included a semi-automated coffee bean plucking machine and three product lines at Factana. I design schematics and PCBs, prepare BOMs, follow ASPICE-aligned processes, and debug at the bench with an oscilloscope and logic analyzer.
