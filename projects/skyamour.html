01Overview

Many phones can't receive Auracast broadcasts on their own because they have no native support for it. That leaves listeners needing extra hardware to join a broadcast.

SkyAmour is an Android app I developed at OpenEcore to close that gap. It enables Auracast listening on phones that don't support it natively, so people can use the phone they already own.

02How it works

  • Auracast broadcastTransmitted audio stream
  • SkyAmour appAndroid, no native Auracast needed
  • ListenerHears the broadcast

  1. A broadcast is on air

    An Auracast transmitter broadcasts an audio stream to anyone in range.

  2. SkyAmour handles the listening

    The app brings Auracast listening to phones that can't do it natively.

  3. The listener hears it

    Audio from the broadcast plays on the listener's own phone.
