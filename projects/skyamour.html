01Overview

Many phones can't receive Auracast Broadcasts on their own because they have no native support for it. Only selected 2023+ flagships, such as the Samsung Galaxy S23 to S25 and Pixel 8 series, include it, and built-in stacks usually play only the first language.

SkyAmour Connect is an Android app I developed at OpenEcore to close that gap. It needs only Bluetooth LE, not an LE Audio phone, and drives Auracast earbuds or speakers through our own BLE control service. Listeners can pick any language, enter the Broadcast PIN and control playback from the app.

02How it works

  • Auracast BroadcastTransmitted audio stream
  • SkyAmour appAndroid, BLE only
  • Earbuds or speakerReceives the audio directly

  1. A Broadcast is on air

    An Auracast Transmitter Broadcasts audio, for example a movie in two languages, to anyone in range.

  2. The app finds and joins it

    Over BLE the app tells the earbuds to scan, lists each Broadcast by name and language, and joins the one the listener picks, with the PIN if one is set.

  3. The audio skips the phone

    The Receiver picks up the Broadcast straight from the Transmitter. The phone only sends commands such as volume, pause, stop and switch language.

04Key features

  • Works without native supportRuns on Android phones with Bluetooth LE. No LE Audio chipset needed.
  • Pick any languageBuilt-in phone stacks play the first language. The app lists every language.
  • Protected BroadcastsType the Broadcast code (PIN) in the app to unlock a private stream.
  • Live controlVolume, pause, stop and switch language without leaving the app.
  • Earbuds or speakerOne app drives both TWS earbuds and a stereo speaker.
