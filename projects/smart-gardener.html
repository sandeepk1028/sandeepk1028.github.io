01Overview

Mowing a lawn by hand is tiring and time-consuming. Smart Gardener is an autonomous robot that cuts the lawn for you.

It navigates the terrain with sensors and controls its own motors, as an eco-friendly alternative to manual mowing.

It was my final-year project for the B.E. in Electronics & Communication at MVJ College of Engineering. The prototype is built on an Arduino with motor drivers and a display.

02How it works

  • SensorsRead the terrain
  • ArduinoDecides where to go
  • Motor driversControl the wheels
  • RobotMoves and cuts

  1. Sense

    Sensors tell the robot about the terrain and obstacles around it.

  2. Decide

    The Arduino processes the readings and picks the next move.

  3. Drive

    Motor drivers turn that decision into wheel motion.
